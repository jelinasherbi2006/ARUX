# Base Paper Analysis

## 1. Selected Base Paper

**Title:** AI-Powered Career Guidance: A Scalable Model for Personalized Recommendations

**Authors:** Minakshi Roy and Kalpana Sharma

**Publisher:** IEEE

**Journal:** IEEE Access

**Year:** 2026

**DOI:** 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3661573

---

## 2. Why This Paper Was Selected

This paper was selected as the base paper for ARUX because it directly addresses
AI-based personalized career guidance and career recommendation.

The paper provides a machine-learning-based foundation for recommending suitable
career pathways according to individual career-interest and profile information.

---

## 3. Problem Addressed

Traditional career guidance can be difficult to personalize because individuals
have different interests, abilities and career preferences.

The paper investigates how machine learning can be used to provide personalized
career recommendations at scale.

---

## 4. Main Approach

The proposed approach uses career-interest information, particularly the RIASEC
model, together with machine-learning techniques to generate personalized career
recommendations.

The study evaluates multiple machine-learning models and compares their
performance for career recommendation.

---

## 5. RIASEC Model

The paper uses the six RIASEC career-interest categories:

- Realistic
- Investigative
- Artistic
- Social
- Enterprising
- Conventional

These categories help represent an individual's career interests.

---

## 6. Machine Learning

The study evaluates machine-learning approaches including:

- Support Vector Machine (SVM)
- XGBoost
- Decision Tree
- Other comparative models

The models are evaluated to determine their effectiveness in personalized
career recommendation.

---

## 7. Dataset

The study uses a large career-related dataset containing more than 80,000
records.

The dataset contains information related to individuals' profiles,
education, employment information, career interests and job preferences.

---

## 8. Evaluation

The paper evaluates the proposed approach using quantitative evaluation
methods.

The study includes:

- Five-fold cross-validation
- Model comparison
- Ablation analysis
- Career recommendation evaluation

These evaluations are used to assess the effectiveness of the proposed
machine-learning models.

---

## 9. Main Output

The main output of the system is a personalized recommendation of suitable
career pathways based on the individual's profile and career interests.

---

## 10. Limitations Relevant to ARUX

The paper identifies limitations related to the use of cross-sectional data
and the difficulty of continuously tracking career development over time.

The study also identifies the need for future work involving longitudinal
career tracking and more adaptive career-guidance systems.

These limitations provide a useful research direction for ARUX.

---

## 11. Base Paper vs ARUX

| Base Paper | ARUX Extension |
|---|---|
| Career profile analysis | Continuous Career DNA |
| RIASEC-based career interests | Career interests + academic performance + skills + learning progress |
| ML-based career recommendation | Dynamic career recommendation |
| Primarily profile-based information | Continuously updated student profile |
| Cross-sectional information | Longitudinal student development |
| Career recommendation | Career recommendation + dynamic skill-gap analysis |
| Career guidance | Personalized learning roadmap |
| Limited continuous adaptation | Continuous feedback-driven adaptation |
| Career recommendation as major output | Student → Learning → Career → Employment lifecycle |

---

## 12. What ARUX Extends

ARUX extends the base-paper direction by proposing a continuously evolving
career-intelligence system.

The proposed ARUX cycle is:

**Student Data → Career DNA → Performance Analysis → Skill-Gap Analysis →
Personalized Learning → Progress Monitoring → Career Recommendation →
Job Matching → Employment Feedback → Career DNA Update**

The goal is to continuously update recommendations as the student's skills,
performance and career development change.

---

## 13. Potential Research Gap

The base paper provides a strong foundation for personalized career
recommendation using machine learning.

ARUX investigates a broader longitudinal approach in which student development,
skill gaps, learning progress, career recommendations and employment outcomes
are connected through a continuous feedback cycle.

The research gap will be further validated through comparison with additional
recent research papers.

---

## 14. Role of the Base Paper in ARUX

The selected IEEE 2026 paper serves as the primary research foundation for
the career-recommendation component of ARUX.

ARUX does not intend to reproduce the base paper. Instead, the base paper's
approach is used as a foundation for investigating an expanded,
continuously adaptive career-intelligence system.
