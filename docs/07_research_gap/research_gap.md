# Research Gap

## 1. Background

Recent research has explored AI-based career recommendation, personalized
career guidance, student performance analysis and personalized learning.

These studies demonstrate that artificial intelligence and machine learning
can support students in making better career and learning decisions.

However, these capabilities are often studied as separate components or
within limited stages of the student's career-development journey.

---

## 2. Observations from Existing Research

The selected base paper provides a machine-learning-based foundation for
personalized career recommendation.

The reference studies further demonstrate:

* Adaptive career guidance using multiple student characteristics.
* Personalized learning-path recommendation based on learner information.
* Use of student performance data for career recommendation.

These studies establish that individual components required for intelligent
career development are technically feasible.

---

## 3. Identified Research Gap

The key research opportunity identified from the literature is the need to
investigate how these capabilities can be connected into a continuously
evolving career-development lifecycle.

Instead of providing a career recommendation only from a current profile,
the proposed direction is to continuously incorporate:

* Academic performance
* Demonstrated skills
* Career interests
* Learning progress
* Skill gaps
* Career requirements
* Job opportunities
* Employment outcomes

into an evolving student career profile.

---

## 4. Proposed ARUX Approach

ARUX proposes a continuously evolving **Career DNA** that represents the
student's current career-related state.

The proposed lifecycle is:

**Student Data → Career DNA → Performance Analysis → Skill-Gap Analysis →
Personalized Learning → Progress Monitoring → Career Recommendation →
Job/Company Matching → Employment Outcome → Career DNA Update**

This creates a feedback-driven cycle rather than a one-time career
recommendation.

---

## 5. Key Difference

The primary research direction of ARUX is the **continuous integration of
student development, learning and career decision-making**.

The system is intended to update recommendations as the student's skills,
performance and learning progress change.

Employment outcomes can provide additional feedback for future career
recommendations.

---

## 6. Research Gap Statement

Existing research has demonstrated AI-based career recommendation, adaptive
career guidance, personalized learning and performance-based prediction.

However, there remains an opportunity to investigate a unified and
continuously evolving framework that connects student performance, dynamic
skill-gap identification, personalized learning, career recommendation,
employment matching and outcome-based feedback.

ARUX proposes to investigate this integrated lifecycle through the concept of
a continuously evolving Career DNA.

---

## 7. Research Questions

### RQ1

Can a continuously updated student profile improve the personalization of
career recommendations compared with a static profile?

### RQ2

Can student performance and demonstrated skills be used to dynamically
identify career-specific skill gaps?

### RQ3

Can identified skill gaps be used to generate personalized learning
recommendations?

### RQ4

Can learning progress continuously influence career recommendations?

### RQ5

Can employment outcomes provide useful feedback for updating future career
recommendations?

---

## 8. Proposed Research Contribution

ARUX proposes an integrated career-development framework that connects:

**Student Profile + Performance + Skills + Learning + Career + Employment**

through a continuously evolving **Career DNA**.

The contribution is therefore investigated at the system-integration and
continuous-feedback level rather than claiming that individual components
such as career recommendation or personalized learning are themselves new.

---

## 9. Research Gap Validation

The identified research gap is a proposed research direction and must be
validated through further experimentation and comparison with existing
systems.

Future work will evaluate whether the continuous Career DNA lifecycle can
provide measurable improvements in personalization, skill-gap identification,
learning recommendations and career outcomes.

---

## 10. Conclusion

The research gap identified for ARUX is the opportunity to investigate a
continuous, feedback-driven career-development system that evolves with the
student.

ARUX aims to connect the stages of:

**Understand → Assess → Identify Gap → Learn → Improve → Recommend → Apply →
Measure Outcome → Update**

into a continuous lifecycle.
