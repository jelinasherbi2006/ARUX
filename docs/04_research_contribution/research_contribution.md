# ARUX — PROPOSED UNIQUE RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION

## 1. Proposed Contribution

ARUX proposes an evolving Career DNA framework that combines multiple student signals, including academic performance, skills, assessments, projects, certifications, learning progress, career goals, interview outcomes, and employment feedback, into a continuously updated career profile.

## 2. Continuous Career Intelligence

Unlike a static resume or a one-time career recommendation system, ARUX is designed to continuously update its understanding of a student as new information and outcomes become available.

## 3. Dynamic Feedback Loop

Student Data
→ Career DNA
→ AI Analysis
→ Skill-Gap Detection
→ Personalized Action
→ Assessment / Interview / Employment Outcome
→ Career DNA Update

This continuous feedback loop allows the system to adapt its recommendations and career-readiness assessment as the student's capabilities and outcomes change.

## 4. Research Direction

The proposed research direction is the dynamic integration of multiple career-related signals with a continuous feedback mechanism to support adaptive skill-gap analysis, personalized guidance, career-readiness assessment, and opportunity matching.

## 5. Novelty Verification

The distinctiveness and novelty of this proposed contribution must be verified through a literature survey, comparison with existing systems, and prior-art review.

Therefore, this document describes ARUX's proposed research contribution and does not yet make an unverified claim that the approach is completely novel.
