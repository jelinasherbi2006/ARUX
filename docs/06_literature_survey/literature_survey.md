# Literature Survey

## 1. Purpose

The literature survey is conducted to understand existing research related to
AI-based career guidance, career recommendation, personalized learning,
student performance analysis and adaptive career development.

The survey helps identify the research gap that ARUX intends to investigate.

---

## 2. Base Paper

### AI-Powered Career Guidance: A Scalable Model for Personalized Recommendations

**Authors:** Minakshi Roy and Kalpana Sharma

**Publisher:** IEEE

**Journal:** IEEE Access

**Year:** 2026

**DOI:** 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3661573

### Role in ARUX

This paper is selected as the primary base paper for ARUX because it provides
a machine-learning-based foundation for personalized career recommendation.

The paper studies career-interest and profile information and applies machine
learning to recommend suitable career pathways.

ARUX uses this research direction as its starting foundation and proposes an
expanded, continuously evolving career-intelligence approach.

---

## 3. Reference Papers

### Reference 1

**Title:** Designing a Precision Career-Guidance Model Based on Student
Psychological Profiling in Higher Education

**Year:** 2026

**Role in the Survey:**

This work demonstrates the use of psychological profiling, academic
information and personal interests for personalized and adaptive career
guidance.

It helps ARUX understand how multiple student characteristics can contribute
to career guidance.

---

### Reference 2

**Title:** Personalized Learning Path Recommendation Based on Underachiever
Profiles

**Year:** 2026

**Publisher:** IEEE

**Role in the Survey:**

This work focuses on learner profiling, knowledge-state analysis and
personalized learning-path recommendation.

It provides supporting research for the personalized learning component of
ARUX.

---

### Reference 3

**Title:** Gamified Career Recommendation System Using Machine Learning

**Year:** 2026

**Publisher:** IEEE

**Role in the Survey:**

This work investigates the use of measurable student activity and performance
information, such as accuracy, response time, retries and difficulty
progression, for career recommendation.

It provides supporting research for using demonstrated student performance
rather than relying only on stated preferences.

## 5. Research Gap

Existing research has demonstrated AI-based career recommendation, adaptive
career guidance, personalized learning-path recommendation and
performance-informed career prediction.

However, there remains an opportunity to investigate a unified, continuously
evolving framework that connects a student's academic and performance
evidence with dynamic skill-gap identification, personalized learning,
career recommendation, employment matching and outcome-based feedback.

ARUX proposes to investigate this continuous Career DNA-driven lifecycle.

---

## 6. Proposed ARUX Research Direction

The proposed ARUX lifecycle is:

**Student Data → Career DNA → Performance Analysis → Skill-Gap Analysis →
Personalized Learning → Progress Monitoring → Career Recommendation →
Job/Company Matching → Employment Outcome → Career DNA Update**

The system is intended to continuously update the student's career profile as
new academic, skill, learning and employment information becomes available.

---

## 7. ARUX Extension Over Existing Research

ARUX does not attempt to reproduce any individual existing system.

Instead, it investigates an integrated approach in which:

* Student information is continuously represented through **Career DNA**.
* Academic and practical performance contribute to the student's evolving
  profile.
* Current capabilities are compared with requirements of potential career
  paths.
* Identified skill gaps influence personalized learning recommendations.
* Learning progress updates the student's skill profile.
* Career recommendations adapt to the student's development.
* Job and company opportunities can be connected to the evolving profile.
* Employment or career outcomes can provide feedback for future
  recommendations.

---

## 8. Research Position of ARUX

The selected base paper provides the primary foundation for the career
recommendation component of ARUX.

The three reference papers provide supporting evidence for adaptive career
guidance, personalized learning and performance-based career recommendation.

Together, these studies help establish the research context from which ARUX
will investigate a continuous, integrated career-development lifecycle.

---

## 9. Conclusion

The literature survey shows that important components of ARUX already exist
individually across recent research.

Therefore, ARUX's research direction is not based on claiming that AI career
recommendation, personalized learning or skill analysis are individually
new.

Instead, the project focuses on investigating how these components can be
integrated into a continuously evolving Career DNA-driven system connecting
student development, learning, career decisions and employment outcomes.

Further research and implementation will be used to validate the effectiveness
and novelty of this proposed approach.
