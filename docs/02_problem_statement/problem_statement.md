ARUX — Problem Statement

     Students today must navigate multiple disconnected platforms for academics, coding practice, skill development, certifications, projects, assessments, interview preparation, and job opportunities. Their career-related information and performance are scattered across these platforms, making it difficult to understand their actual strengths, identify the skills they are missing, decide what they should learn next, measure their career readiness, and determine which opportunities are suitable for them. Existing solutions generally address individual parts of this journey rather than providing a continuous, personalized view of the student's career development. Therefore, there is a need for an AI-powered Career Intelligence Platform that can integrate these diverse signals, build an evolving Career DNA, identify personalized skill gaps, guide development, measure readiness, learn from outcomes, and connect students with suitable career opportunities.
