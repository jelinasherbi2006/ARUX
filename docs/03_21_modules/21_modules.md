# ARUX — 21 CORE MODULES

## 1. Student Profile Intelligence
- Stores academic, skill, project, certification, and career information.
- Creates a unified student profile.

## 2. Career DNA Engine
- Builds a dynamic representation of the student's career capabilities.
- Includes strengths, weaknesses, skills, growth, and career readiness.

## 3. Career DNA World Rank
- Compares Career DNA using defined evaluation criteria.
- Shows the student's relative ranking and progress.

## 4. Resume Intelligence
- Analyzes resumes using NLP.
- Extracts skills, education, projects, and experience.

## 5. Skill Extraction & Verification
- Identifies technical and professional skills.
- Builds a structured skill profile.

## 6. Skill-Gap Analysis
- Compares current skills with target career requirements.
- Identifies missing or weak skills.

## 7. Career Recommendation
- Suggests suitable career roles based on the student's profile.
- Explains why a particular role is recommended.

## 8. Personalized Learning Roadmap
- Suggests what to learn next.
- Adapts based on progress and performance.

## 9. Smart Resource Recommendation
- Recommends suitable learning topics and resources.
- Helps students decide which platform or resource to use next.

## 10. Assessment & Weakness Detection
- Conducts tests and assessments.
- Identifies weak topics from performance.

## 11. Performance Prediction
- Predicts future readiness or performance trends.
- Detects areas requiring improvement.

## 12. Project & Portfolio Intelligence
- Analyzes student projects and portfolio development.
- Suggests improvements based on target roles.

## 13. Certification Intelligence
- Tracks certifications and their relevance.
- Identifies useful certifications for career goals.

## 14. Job Readiness Score
- Measures overall readiness for a selected career or job.
- Updates continuously as the student improves.

## 15. Job & Opportunity Matching
- Matches students with suitable jobs, internships, or opportunities.
- Matches skills and Career DNA with role requirements.

## 16. Company / Recruiter Portal
- Allows authorized companies to view relevant student Career DNA.
- Helps recruiters discover suitable candidates.

## 17. Interview Preparation
- Provides role-based interview preparation.
- Tracks preparation progress.

## 18. Interview Outcome & Failure Analyzer
- Analyzes interview results or feedback.
- Identifies possible weaknesses and recommends improvements.

## 19. Continuous Feedback Loop
- Updates recommendations using new assessments, projects, interviews, and outcomes.
- Ensures ARUX evolves with the student.

## 20. Career Trend Intelligence
- Tracks changing skill and career trends.
- Helps students prepare for emerging requirements.

## 21. ARUX AI Career Assistant
- Acts as the central conversational intelligence layer.
- Answers questions and guides the student through their career journey.
